# AI Carb Load Tracker: Quick Start Guide

Your AI carb-loading coach helps you plan for marathon day, log what you eat and drink, and track your daily carbohydrate goal. It works with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. No technical experience is needed.

## 1. Set up your coach

Set up before carb loading begins:
- **2-day load:** Set up 3 days before race day.
- **3-day load:** Set up 4 days before race day.

1. Sign in to ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and start a new chat.
2. Open [`coach_prompt.md`](coach_prompt.md) and click **Copy raw file** (the button with two overlapping squares).
3. Paste the instructions into your new chat and send them.
4. Answer the coach's questions about your race, body weight, food preferences, and other setup details.

Your coach will create a daily carbohydrate target and a plan. A *carbohydrate target* is the amount of carbs you're aiming to eat each day.

**Keep using the same chat** throughout your carb load so your coach can refer to earlier entries.

## 2. Log what you eat and drink

Whenever you eat or drink something, tell your coach what it was and roughly how much. **You don't need to add a date or time to every entry**—just describe what you've had naturally.

**Examples:**
> I had a plain bagel with honey for breakfast.

> I drank one bottle of sports drink. The label says 34 g of carbs per bottle.

Your coach should add the item to your log, update your daily total, and suggest what to eat next. Check its response and point out anything that looks wrong or was missed.

## 3. Get more accurate results

- **Packaged foods and drinks:** Type the “Total Carbohydrate” number from the nutrition label and how much you consumed. Label information is usually more reliable than a photo estimate.
- **Restaurant meals:** Describe what you ordered or send a photo. Photo estimates aren't exact; portion details help.
- **Choosing from a menu:** Ask the coach to compare two or three dishes before you order. Tell it what you actually ate so it can log the right meal.
- **Can't upload a photo?** Type the food, portion size, and any nutrition information you have.

Photo uploads may be limited on free AI accounts, and limits can change. Save photos for meals or menus that are difficult to describe; typing usually works well.

## 4. Check your progress

After logging food or a drink, take a quick look at the coach's response:
- **Food log:** Is the item listed correctly?
- **Daily total / target:** How much have you logged compared with your goal?
- **“Next up”:** What does the coach suggest eating next?

AI can make mistakes, including arithmetic errors. If a number looks wrong, ask the coach to show its calculation and recalculate the total from your logged foods and drinks.

## If something goes wrong

- **The coach forgot an entry:** Tell it what you ate and ask it to add the item to today's log.
- **The total looks wrong:** Ask it to show its math and check the portions and label values.
- **The coach forgot earlier meals:** Make sure you're using the same chat you used for setup.
- **You can't upload a photo:** Type the food and portion size instead.

## Carb targets and safety

The tracker uses general sports-nutrition guidance to suggest targets. The guidance in this project is approximately 10–12 grams of carbs per kilogram of body weight per day for a 2-day load, or about 8 grams per kilogram per day over 3 days. Your coach's target is a guide, not a guarantee that the plan is right for you.

This tool provides general information, not medical advice. AI estimates can be wrong, so check labels and use your judgment. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have a history of disordered eating, speak with a doctor or sports dietitian before carb loading. On race morning, stick to foods and gels you've already practiced with during training.
