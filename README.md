# AI Carb Load Tracker: How to Use It

A free AI coach that plans your marathon carb load and keeps a running total of what you eat. It works in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Setup takes about 5 minutes. Logging takes about 1 minute per meal or snack.

## Start (the evening before your load begins)

Start 3 days before the race for a 2-day load, or 4 days before for a 3-day load. Then your plan is ready before the first breakfast.

1. Open ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), Claude (claude.ai), or Gemini (gemini.google.com). **Sign in first.** A free account works. Without signing in, the app does not save your chat (you lose your totals) and may not let you send photos.
2. Open [coach_prompt.md](coach_prompt.md). Click the **Copy raw file** button (two overlapping squares, top right of the file). Start a new chat, paste the prompt, and press send.
3. Answer the coach's questions: race date, weight, 2-day or 3-day load, food dislikes, and whether to track fluids. If the app shows a form, fill it in. Otherwise, type your answers.

The coach then gives you your daily carb target and a plan for each day.

## Every day of the load

- **Use the same chat for all days.** A new chat forgets your totals. Bookmark the chat or find it in the sidebar.
- **Start each entry with the day and time.** Example: "Day 1, 8 am: 1 bagel with 2 tbsp honey." The coach uses the time to tell you what to eat next.
- **Send everything you eat or drink.** For packaged food, type the label's carbs. For restaurant food, send a photo of your plate or the menu.
- **Read the "Next up" line.** It tells you what to eat next to stay on track.
- **Check the math.** The coach shows a full table after each entry. If a number looks wrong, say so. AI can make arithmetic mistakes.

## Free plans: save your photos

Free plans limit photo uploads. In testing, ChatGPT Free stopped uploads after 3 photos and said to try again the next day. Use photos only where typing does not work:

- **Packaged food or drinks:** type the label instead. Example: "Powerade, 34 g carbs per bottle, drank 1 bottle."
- **Restaurant plates and menus:** use a photo. There is no label to type.
- If a photo does not upload, type what you ate. The coach asks for the numbers it needs.

## Tips

- Label numbers give the most accurate totals. Plate photos are estimates.
- Send a menu photo before you order. The coach picks the best carb choices for that meal. On a free plan, each menu photo uses one of your limited uploads. To save uploads, type 2–3 dishes you are considering and ask which one is best.
- Put something of known size in plate photos, such as a 20 oz bottle.
- Juice, sports drinks, and Italian ice are an easy way to get carbs when you are tired of eating.

## Important

This coach gives general information, not medical advice. If you have diabetes, kidney disease, a stomach or bowel condition, are pregnant, or have a history of disordered eating, talk to a doctor or sports dietitian first. On race morning, eat only foods and gels you have practiced in training.

Carb targets follow published sports-nutrition guidance: 10–12 g of carbs per kg of body weight per day for 36–48 hours, or about 8 g/kg per day over 3 days.
